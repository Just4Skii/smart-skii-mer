Smart Skii-mer — quick start

What it does
  Watches memory, puts inactive apps into Windows Efficiency mode, and lets you clean up
  with one click or a hotkey (default Ctrl+Alt+D). Your active app, games and system
  processes are never touched or closed.

First run
  The dashboard opens. Everything works offline. The assistant is optional:
  Settings > AI access lets you paste your own OpenRouter key (starts with sk-or-).
  Without a key, a built-in checker still gives advice.

Phone remote (optional)
  Turn it on in Settings > Phone remote. On the same Wi-Fi, open the address shown
  in the app on your phone and enter the 4-digit code.
  If the app says the remote is blocked, run the installer again as admin and keep
  the "Allow my phone to control this PC" option ticked.

Privacy
  No accounts, no telemetry. Nothing leaves the PC until you press "Analyse my PC",
  and then only a small system summary is sent to the AI provider you configured.

Uninstall
  Settings > Apps > Smart Skii-mer > Uninstall. Your settings stay in
  %APPDATA%\Smart Skii-mer unless you remove them manually.
